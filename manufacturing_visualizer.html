Editable Workcenter Parameters & Real-Time Analytics
Part Mapping: Each customer has 4 part numbers on record, but internally every part is classified into just 2 physical types — Green or Red — and it's that Green/Red type, not the specific part number, that actually moves through the line.
Customer Order Rule: A customer's next order will not be loaded into the line/queue until that customer's earlier order has been fully delivered — only one order per customer can be in-process at any time.
Workflow: Before is the current-state line as-is. Improvements is where you list proposed changes, their cost impact, and the new workcenter time values they produce. After visualizes the line running on those new values, so you can compare it directly against Before.
